Please see the requirements below regarding capturing and presenting the Adverse Party Liability:

1.     Ability to present the Adverse Party Liability Determination with a financial summary for easy viewing

a.     Initial recommendation is to include the Adverse Party Liability information in the Claim Details pop-up you demoed

b.     I have created a corresponding field in the Checklist to capture the Adverse Party Liability percentage, which can be used to populate the display. Question: Can we apply a rule so that this field accepts only numeric values?

c.     In the Claim Detail pop-up, please present the financial amounts by coverage, including:

·       Amount paid by coverage

·       Expected recovery by coverage (system calculated based on the adverse liability %)

·       Amount recovered by coverage

·       Expected amount balance by coverage

As a claim progresses and negotiations or recoveries occur, the expected amount balance may change. For example, when we negotiate for less than the expected recovery or when the adverse carrier pays more than anticipated.

In these situations, we do not want to alter the original expected recovery value. Instead, the expected amount balance should automatically update to reflect what we are actually pursuing at that point in time.

Currently, the team calculates this manually, which introduces room for error. Presenting this data correctly in the system would greatly increase accuracy and efficiency. We also want the user to be aware when the Expected Recovery Balance has been impacted by negotiations. To avoid displaying excessive information directly on the Claim Details page, a negotiation pop-up or expandable section could be used to provide additional detail when applicable.

We also need to categorize coverages by coverage type. This may not need to appear directly on the Claim Details page, but it is important to mention since we are discussing coverages and the Expected Recovery Balance. 

Example:

Collision and Rental → Property Damage coverage type
Personal Injury Protection → Injury coverage type
Categorizing coverages allows the user to easily view totals by coverage type. At adverse carriers, there is typically a Property Damage adjuster and a separate Injury adjuster, so being able to see the total requested amounts by coverage type would be highly beneficial.

Additionally, categorizing by coverage type will support the correct determination of the Statute of Limitations, which varies based on the type of coverage and the applicable recovery rights.
