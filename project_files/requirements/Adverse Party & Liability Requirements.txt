Adverse Party & Liability Page Requirements 
Document Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the requirements for the Adverse Party & Liability page, including the capture of all adverse parties and the organizations that represent them (such as adverse carriers, third-party administrators, and legal counsel). These requirements establish the data, behaviors, and rules needed to support accurate liability assessment and ensure complete, reliable information for subrogation and recovery activities.
Page Title – Rename to Adverse Party & Liability (renamed from Party & Liability) 

Liability Summary Section

The Liability Summary Section should be presented by Loss Type, and the requirements outlined in this section apply specifically at the Loss Type level. Individual liability fields do not require Line of Business or Loss Type values as liability follows the claim. The summary view is organized by Subrogable Loss Type for reporting and user clarity.

Field Name - Total Coverage Amount Sum of all party coverage amounts

Purpose: Displays the total amount of adverse coverage available by summing the coverage limits for all identified adverse parties. This value supports evaluation of potential subrogation recovery and helps determine the financial exposure and recovery strategy.

Recommendation:  Although this is a primary field on the page, adverse carriers typically do not provide coverage-limit information unless a limits issue is identified. Therefore, it is recommended that this field not be displayed as a prominent calculation on the page, as the value may be unavailable or unreliable in most cases.  

Field Name - Combined Adverse Liability % (renamed from Total Liability Percentage Sum of all party liability percentages) 

Purpose: Displays the combined liability percentage assigned to all adverse parties. This total allows users to verify that liability allocations across parties are complete and accurately reflect the overall fault determination for the claim.

Requirements:

The Combined Adverse Liability Percentage is calculated by summing the liability percentages assigned to all adverse parties recorded on the claim. 

The system currently performs this calculation by adding each individual party’s liability percentage and displaying the aggregated total.

Field Name - Combined Recovery (renamed from: Total Paid Sum of paid across liabilities)

Purpose: Represents the total amount recovered across all liability entries, providing a consolidated view of subrogation recoveries made against adverse parties

Requirements:

The Combined Recovery amount is calculated by summing all recovered amounts recorded across the liability entries for the claim. 

The system currently performs this calculation by adding each individual recovery value and displaying the aggregated total as the Combined Recovery amount.

Field Name - Expected Recovery Balance

Purpose: Represents the remaining amount that can still be recovered after accounting for all recoveries made to date.

Requirement:

The Expected Recovery Balance is calculated by applying each adverse party’s liability percentage to the Total Paid amount to determine the expected recovery, then subtracting the Combined Recovery amount. 

The system calculates this by: (Liability % × Total Paid) – Total Recovered = Expected Recovery Balance

Linked Party Section

Description: Requirements related to adding parties will be defined in the Add Parties function, and once an entity is added, the UI will display all corresponding details in this section. Currently, the newly designed system captures various adverse entities and facilitators such as adverse carriers, attorneys, or third-party administrators. However, it does not provide a way to associate a facilitator with the specific entity they represent. To support accurate liability assignment and clear representation, users should be able to identify which facilitators are linked to which adverse entities. For example, Allstate should be identifiable as the insurance carrier for Jane Doe, and that same entity may also have attorney representation that must be linked accordingly. It is recommended that adverse entities be added first and assigned liability, followed by adding facilitators under the corresponding entity to clearly display which facilitator represents which adverse party. Perhaps there can be an “Add Facilitator” button much like the “Add Liability” button is available within the party.

**For clarity within this requirement, an adverse entity refers to the specific person or organization involved in the loss and is generally unique to a single claim in the system. Facilitators, such as insurance carriers, attorneys, or third-party administrators, are parties that commonly recur across multiple claims. This distinction ensures that adverse entities are added as claim-specific records, while facilitators can be added in Party Management and and linked to those entities as needed to represent their role in the claim.

Add Party

Field Name – Party Role

Purpose: To capture and display all roles an adverse entity holds within a loss, allowing the system to assign multiple roles to the same entity when applicable. This ensures accurate representation of the entity’s involvement in the claim.

A single party may serve in multiple capacities within a loss event. Party roles help define the entity’s involvement, responsibility, or relationship to the claim. In certain scenarios—such as an auto loss—an adverse entity may hold multiple roles simultaneously (e.g., the same individual may be both the vehicle owner and a passenger at the time of the accident).

Requirements:

Support assigning one or more roles to the same adverse entity.

Drop-down selections should be maintained in Party Role table.

Field Name – Party

Purpose: Captures the legal identity of the party, whether an individual or a business entity.

Requirements:

Support Parties that are either individuals or business entities.



For individuals, name is First Name, Middle Initial (optional), and Last Name as separate fields.


For data feed clients:

Present the value exactly as provided in the feed.
For individuals, claim system standards typically store name components as separate fields; the system must map each component (first, middle, last) into the corresponding stored fields in Manifest.
If data feed is in place, user to still have the ability to add additional parties manually.

For manual entry: 

Users are to enter First Name (compulsory), Middle Initial (optional), Last Name (compulsory) OR
Business Name in a single field

Field Name – Representative (Optional)

Purpose: To identify an optional representative associated with the adverse entity. This representative may be an individual acting on behalf of an adverse company (e.g., employer or company contact), a person holding power of attorney for an individual, or a parent/guardian representing a minor. This field ensures the system can capture and display the appropriate point of contact or authorized party connected to the adverse entity.

Requirements:

This name can be freeform and does not need to be added as a party.

Field Name – Liability Percentage

Purpose: Assigns the liability percentage to the adverse entity.

Requirements: 

Accepts a manually entered percentage.

The system currently performs this function as expected.



Field Name – Notes

Purpose: Allows the user to enter any additional information related to the adverse entity that is not captured in a predefined field. This supports documenting context, clarifications, or details that do not fit within structured data fields.

Add Liability Fields

Field Name: Line of Business

Liability assignment applies to the claim as a whole and is not dependent on Line of Business. Therefore the Line of Business field can be removed from the Liability section, as it is not required for determining or displaying liability information.

Field Name: Loss Type

Liability assignment applies to the claim as a whole and is not dependent on Loss Type. Therefore, the Loss Type field can be removed from the Liability section, as they are not required for determining or displaying liability information.

Field Name: Coverage Amount

Purpose: To record the coverage limit provided by each facilitator representing the adverse entity. Coverage information may not always be available, as adverse carriers typically do not supply coverage limits unless a coverage or limits issue exists.

Requirements:

The Coverage Amount is to be captured at the facilitator level, as an adverse entity may have multiple insurance carriers or representatives. 
Because coverage varies by facilitator, this field must be stored and maintained on the facilitator record rather than the adverse entity record. 

Finalization of this requirement is dependent on the implementation of linking facilitators to adverse entities rather than treating facilitators as stand-alone parties. 

Field Name: Amount Recovered (renamed from: Amount Paid)

Purpose: To track the total amount recovered from an adverse entity or its associated facilitators. 

Requirements: 

Calculate the Amount Recovered based on the sum of all recovery transactions entered for the claim from the entity/party.

Captured by Loss Type, with totals calculated separately for each applicable loss type.

The system shall update the Amount Recovered value automatically whenever new recovery transactions are added, modified, or removed.

For Discussion – Tracking Recoveries at the Coverage-Paid Level

The system must track recoveries at the coverage level based on the coverage amounts paid by the client. A detailed coverage breakdown is required to support subrogation activities and ensure accurate recovery evaluation. From a business perspective, this information needs to be captured; however, the placement and design of this functionality is to be determined with the system architect. Further discussion is needed to evaluate whether this capability should remain within the current Adverse Party and Liability page or be implemented on a dedicated page that may better support detailed tracking of:

Amount paid by the client at the coverage level (coverage paid)
Amount agreed to pay by the adverse entity or facilitator (settlement amount).
Amount received from the adverse party at the coverage level
Remaining balance based on expected recovery at the coverage level

This functionality is tied to coverage paid by the client and may be better supported through a purpose-built recovery tracking section rather than within the general adverse party/liability framework.

Field Name: Notes

Purpose: Allows the user to enter any additional information related to liability that is not captured in a predefined field. This supports documenting context, clarifications, or details that do not fit within structured data fields.

Additional Requirements Needed for Adding an Entity

From a business perspective, not all entity information needs to be displayed in the primary UI view. The system must capture several components of information for an entity; however, only key details need to be visible by default. Full entity information should remain accessible when needed (e.g., in edit mode or through an expanded view or from a sub-page), while the decision on how best to present or reveal additional details will be determined by the system architect.

Address Status

Purpose: Identifies the usability or validity of the address.

Requirements:

Allow the user to flag each address as one of the following:

Valid Address
Mailing Address
Undeliverable
The system must allow "Unknown" as a selectable address status.
This option is primarily used for data-feed records where the feed provides an address but does not indicate the address status)
Only one address can be flagged as the Valid Address at any time.  The Valid Address is the address to which system-generated or manually generated mail will be sent.

Multiple Addresses

Purpose: Allows the system to store alternative and historical and addresses.

Requirements:

Users to be able to add multiple addresses per party.

Only one address may be flagged as Valid Address.

In summary view, the system should display only the valid address(es) associated with the party.
In edit mode, the system shall display all addresses, valid and invalid, so the user can review, update, or correct them as needed.

Allow multiple phone numbers to be added.

Allow the user to flag each address as one of the following:

Valid
Disconnected

This option is primarily used for data-feed records where the feed provides a phone number but does not indicate the status)
